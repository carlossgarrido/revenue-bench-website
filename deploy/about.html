We have. Revenue Bench was founded by people who have installed sales systems and built and exited revenue teams. We assess talent the way an operator would, so we recognize what predicts performance long before it shows up in the numbers.

What we believe

The principles behind every search.

01

Assess before you hire

Interviewing well is not the same as performing. We measure will to sell, sales DNA, and process fit with Objective Management Group, which reports predictive validity in the 95 percent range, before anyone makes an offer. OMG partner research puts an unaided hiring read at picking a top performer about one time in five.

02

Onboarding is where hires are won or lost

The first 90 days decide whether a hire performs or stalls. So a dedicated coach works with the hire and the manager weekly, every time. We do not hand off and hope.

03

Specialists, not generalists

We do one thing: revenue talent. Sellers, leaders, enablement, and RevOps. We do not staff every function, because depth in the role is what makes the match right.

04

We win when the hire works

Our replacement guarantee puts our name on the outcome. If a hire leaves or is not performing inside the window, we keep replacing until a hire works out, at no additional fee. The window is 90 days for sellers and GTM roles, and 6 to 12 months for sales leaders.

Founder-led, on purpose

You work with the operators, not a handoff.

Revenue Bench is a founder-led firm, sister to Performance Edge and Swanston Growth Advisors. When you engage us, you work directly with the people whose names are on the door, the same people who have built the systems and the teams. Every conversation goes straight to the founders, and every search carries their standard for what good looks like.
