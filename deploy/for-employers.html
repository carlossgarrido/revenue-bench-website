The cost of getting it wrong

A wrong sales hire is among the most expensive mistakes a company can make.

Most teams post a role, read resumes, run interviews, and choose whoever interviews best. Interview skill does not predict performance in a quota-carrying role. Interviews still matter, but the decision gets stronger when selling candidates are assessed against the role.

$200K to $250K
The cost of a single mis-hire in a revenue role is our working figure, counting base, draw, lost pipeline, ramp time, and the management hours spent on someone who should not have been hired. Revenue Bench was built to change that math.

Questions employers ask

Working with Revenue Bench, answered.

What roles do you place?

The full revenue engine. Sellers from SDR and BDR through enterprise AE, sales leadership from Sales Manager to VP Sales and CRO, plus Customer Success, Sales Enablement, RevOps, and GTM talent. Every placement is a permanent, full-time role. The roles and terms table on this page gives the assessment and guarantee window for each role family.

How do you reduce the risk of a mis-hire?

The shortlist starts from a pre-vetted bench. Candidates for selling and sales-leadership roles are evaluated with Objective Management Group, every candidate is screened by an operator for fit with your process, and each placement includes a 90-day onboarding coach and a replacement guarantee if the hire does not work out inside the guarantee window.

How long does a search take?

Kickoff to shortlist commonly runs 2 to 4 weeks when the bench covers the role, and a pure targeted search can take longer. Timing varies by role, seniority, and how much targeted search a role needs beyond the bench. See the stage-by-stage timeline for the full process.

How are your fees structured?

Our fee is one clear percentage of the hire's first-year compensation, backed by the guarantee. Senior and confidential searches, including CRO and VP of Sales roles, run as an engaged, retained-style search. Individual-contributor roles can run on contingency. The structure follows the role rather than a one-size rate card, and our guide to sales recruiter fees explains it in full.

What happens if the hire does not work out?

The onboarding coach meets the hire and the hiring manager weekly for the first 90 days, and exists to give a read on the hire's fit and aptitude inside that window, so a mis-hire is identified early and its impact contained. If the hire does not work out inside the guarantee window, we run the search again at no additional fee and keep replacing until a hire works out, with no limit on re-runs. The window runs from the hire's start date: 90 days for sellers and GTM roles, and 6 to 12 months for sales leaders. The guarantee claim and the employment decision are two separate things: ending someone's employment follows your own documentation, HR process, and counsel under the employment law that applies, and nothing in the guarantee changes that. Our guide explains what to do when a sales hire is not working out.

Which roles does the replacement guarantee cover, and for how long?

The guarantee covers every permanent, full-time placement and runs from the hire's start date. The window is 90 days for sellers from SDR and BDR through enterprise AE and for Customer Success, Sales Enablement, RevOps and GTM roles, and 6 to 12 months for sales managers, front-line leaders, VPs of Sales and CROs, set in the agreement. If a hire does not work out inside the window, Revenue Bench runs the search again at no additional fee and keeps replacing until a hire works out, with no limit on re-runs and no refund.

What does the 90-day coaching include?

Every placement includes a dedicated onboarding coach who meets the new hire weekly and the hiring manager weekly for the first 90 days, and reports to the hiring manager. The coach follows a defined protocol that Revenue Bench does not publish. Its purpose is a read on the hire's fit and aptitude inside those 90 days, so a mis-hire is identified early and its impact contained.
