Miami runs a higher share of its jobs in sales than the country, with the concentration sitting in real estate, insurance, financial services and travel. The occupations that describe a complex-cycle B2B seller sit at or below the national concentration.

What the numbers say about hiring a B2B seller in Miami

The Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metro employed 304,600 people in sales and related occupations in May 2025. Those jobs represented 10.8% of local employment, compared with 8.6% nationally, giving the metro a location quotient of 1.25.

A location quotient is a concentration measure where 1.00 means the Miami metro has the same share of its jobs in that occupation as the country does. A result above 1.00 means the occupation takes a larger share locally. A result below 1.00 means it takes a smaller share.

The local concentration is strongest in real estate, insurance, financial services and travel. The categories closest to complex technical B2B selling are thinner: sales engineers sit at 0.54, and representatives selling technical and scientific products sit at 0.92.

For an employer, the broad sales count confirms that Miami has sales talent. The occupation-level split shows which backgrounds are directly relevant and which require a deliberate conversion test.

What these numbers cannot tell you

  • Annual wages are the BLS mean hourly wage times 2,080 hours. They are not the same as on-target earnings for a commissioned seller, so a quota-carrying package with variable pay can sit well above the mean without being an outlier.
  • The estimates exclude self-employed workers, which understates real estate and travel selling in particular.
  • A location quotient measures how concentrated an occupation is locally. It says nothing about quality, and nothing about whether a given individual can sell in a complex cycle.
  • The occupation categories are broad, so two people inside one BLS row can be doing entirely different jobs.

The adjacent pools, and what transfers

Revenue Bench uses the following framework to evaluate possible conversions. It is our own framework rather than published research, and no statistic is attached to it.

Employment in the financial activities sector of the three-county Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach area, the same geography as the occupation table above, fell by 5,600 jobs, or 2.5%, in the year to June 2026, the largest sector decline in that area, according to the BLS Miami, FL, Area Economic Summary, updated 3 August 2026. Financial activities covers finance, insurance and real estate together rather than securities selling alone. A sector decline is a supply signal rather than a measure of how many quota-carrying sellers are available.

What local wage movement does to a budget set last year

Wages and salaries across the Miami-Port St. Lucie-Fort Lauderdale CSA rose 3.5% in the twelve months to June 2026, compared with 3.1% nationally. That is a wider geography than the three-county occupation table above, and it covers all occupations rather than sales alone. The BLS release was published 4 August 2026.

When a local recruiter matters for a sales hire, and when it does not

A local recruiter matters when

  • The role requires being in front of South Florida buyers or covering a territory that is physically here.
  • The hire must be selected against how business is done in this market, including selling in Spanish where the role requires it.
  • You want the search owner in the same time zone and able to sit across the table.

A local recruiter does not matter when

  • The role is fully remote and the buyer is national.
  • The candidate pool is national by nature, as it often is for a sales engineer.
  • The search constraint is the assessment bar rather than geography.

If the constraint is national, a local office is not the reason to choose a firm.

When a role requires selling in Spanish

Some South Florida sales roles require selling in Spanish, and where that is true it is load-bearing rather than a preference. We publish no percentage, because no figure we could verify to a primary source covers it.

When a role requires selling in Spanish, the requirement goes into the role profile and is tested during the operator screen against a real buyer conversation. We do not take it from a resume line. The broader selection method is covered in how to assess a salesperson.
