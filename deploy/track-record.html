What carries from those engagements into a search

  • Assessment before offer. In the Performance Edge manufacturer rebuild every existing seat was assessed and rehiring ran against the assessment rather than resumes. The same instrument is used on every Revenue Bench candidate for a selling or sales-leadership role.
  • A system to be hired into. The Performance Edge engagements installed a selling method, a weekly sales-leadership routine, and pipeline reporting to ownership. A Revenue Bench search establishes what system the hire will inherit, and the onboarding coach works to that system for the first 90 days.
  • Evidence across two years. The Performance Edge enterprise software engagement ran across two consecutive years, and the second year is on the record alongside the first.

Published commitments

  • Within 90 days of the hire's start date, the client can call for one free re-run of the search, at no additional fee and with no conditions. Whether the hire is working is the client's call. The full guarantee terms are agreed before the search begins.
  • The assessment used on selling and sales-leadership candidates runs through Swanston Growth Advisors.
  • The 90-day onboarding coach meets the hire weekly and reports to the hiring manager.
  • The search process and its stage-by-stage timing are published in full.

The limits of this record

  • No client on this page is named, and none has agreed to be named.
